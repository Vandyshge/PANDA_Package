Citing PANDA

Cite the original PANDA method and the studies relevant to your geometry or analysis.

Original method

Semenchuk, Kondratyuk, and Kopanichuk introduced PANDA in Colloids and Surfaces A, volume 708, article 135994. Include the package version or commit used in your methods.

BibTeX
@article{semenchuk2025panda,
  author  = {Semenchuk, A. A. and Kondratyuk, N. D. and Kopanichuk, I. V.},
  title   = {{PANDA}: Predicting angle from nanoscale density analysis},
  journal = {Colloids and Surfaces A: Physicochemical and Engineering Aspects},
  year    = {2025},
  volume  = {708},
  pages   = {135994},
  doi     = {10.1016/j.colsurfa.2024.135994}
}

Dynamic contact angles

PANDA-D: Interface-free determination of two dynamic contact angles (preprint, 2026). The two-angle extension jointly analyzes density and layerwise center-of-mass displacement.
