What PANDA supports

The name stands for Predicting Angle from Nanoscale Density Analysis. The library covers slit, triangular, square, and cylindrical pores. The shape catalog identifies which models can fit density and which supply surface terms for stability diagrams.

Explore the related PANDA Polygon geometry models and the PANDA Dynamic two-angle method.
