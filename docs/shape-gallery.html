3D shape gallery

Explore interface morphologies in slit, triangular, square, and cylindrical pores.

Check the package capabilities for density fitting and surface terms. Cards marked “Formula draft” show geometries from the research formulas.

Graphite marks the solid walls, dark brown the wetting phase, and blue the non-wetting phase. The contact angle is measured through the blue phase. The volume fraction refers to the full pore, including for cutaway views.
