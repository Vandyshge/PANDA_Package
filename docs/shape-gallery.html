3D shape gallery

Explore interface morphologies in slit, triangular, square, and cylindrical pores.

Check the package capabilities for density fitting and surface terms. Cards marked “Formula draft” show geometries from the research formulas. “Geometry preview” marks a standalone reconstruction.

Graphite marks the solid walls, dark brown the wetting phase, and blue the non-wetting phase. The contact angle is measured through the blue phase. The volume fraction refers to the full pore, including for cutaway views.

For polygonal pores, w counts walls touched and c counts corners occupied by the blue phase. In a square pore, filament_w2c0 touches adjacent walls and filament_w2c0_opp touches opposite walls.
